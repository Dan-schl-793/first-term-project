Hello player. In this game you will be playing a royal knight of the
kingdom Krylo, the kingdom has been overrun with evil ancient beasts
cast upon it by the rival civilisation The Zoli led by their leader Yohl.
It is your job to extinguish the land of these monsters and re-gain control of the casle
All your fellow knights fell in battle trying to keep the
enemy out of the town walls so it is up to you and you alone to

along the way and you must decide whether what they bring to the
party outweighs what they cost you. Be careful, there's a reason all
that came before you fell to these beasts. They are not to be
trifled with.
