To play this game you must have all the txt files installed and run 'python Text_based_game.py' in the terminal.

Krylo: The Lost Kingdom is a text based adventure game about a royal knight whose goal it is to free his home from the grasp of his enemies that have overrun it. Soon after his appointment to protect the kingdom, it fell under attack from a rival civilisation who had uncovered ancient technology allowing them to have an edge in battle. You and your fellow knights worked together to hold them off and for the most part defeat them but you did not entirely wipe out this ancient uncovered technology due to only recently having discovered a means to destroy it. These highly advanced beasts are now hidden around the reckoned kingdom and it's your job to rid your home of their presence. In this game you play as the last royal knight left making choices
of what enemies to attack or avoid and fight later, you must also decide who to allow to join your team. You will be able to make many choices along his journey which affect the outcome of the game and whether you end up freeing the kingdom of these ancient beasts or if you and your team die trying.
